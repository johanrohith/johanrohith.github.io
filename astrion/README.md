# Astrion — Johan Rohith

Astrion is a one-page cinematic personal site built as a journey through three worlds: the universe, the digital world, and literature. Its ending returns to the first star, turning the opening into a loop.

## Experience
- **Universe:** family relationships, ideas about dimensions and timelines, and Uravu.
- **Digital world:** Liora, MCC ReLoop, and Forest Explorer.
- **Literature:** Echoes of the End and the evolving fictional universe, Astrion.
- **Cycle:** a quiet personal introduction and links to connect.

ASTRA appears only as a small recurring easter egg. It is presented as an imagined AI-companion concept, not a functional product.

## Interaction
Move with the Enter/continue controls, mouse wheel, touch gestures, arrow keys, or the chapter markers. Ambient sound is optional and off by default. The reduced-motion control and the browser preference are supported.

## Files
The live page is self-contained in `index.html`; `favicon.svg` is its small custom star mark. There is no build step or framework requirement.
